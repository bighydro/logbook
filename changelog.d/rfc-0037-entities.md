### Added
- RFC 0037 (draft), *Entities*: `entity/v1`, the canonical org, idea, place or asset under the owner's control,
  with an observed→canonical review per kind (`review KIND`, the shape of `people review`) and the entity
  page's common sections; `status/v1`, the owner's disposition of an org (`hold`, `add`, `sell`, `let-go`,
  `exited`, `passed`: a passed company keeps its thread and its reason) or an idea (`spark`, `exploring`,
  `build`, `built`, `killed`), proposed by the dream and written only when the owner answers; what the
  Manifest and Ideas shelves show; tiers, dedupe keys and twelve open questions. Documentation only: no
  command, line or reader changes yet ([rfcs/0037-entities.md](rfcs/0037-entities.md)).
