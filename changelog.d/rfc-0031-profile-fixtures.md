### Profiles and spec
- One conformance fixture per frozen payload profile under `conformance/profiles/<profile>/` (RFC 0031, SPEC §6.1): a one-line
  record, the line's canonical form and hash, the `show` row and the `day` and `days` JSON, generated by
  `conformance/make_profiles.py` and held byte for byte by the tests. The nineteen RFCs say *frozen for v1.0* in their status lines.
