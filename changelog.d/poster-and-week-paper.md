### Added
- `logbook year YYYY --poster [--sheet A2|A3] [--html PATH]` (`logbook/year_poster.py`, `docs/print.md`): the year on one sheet — twelve columns of thirty-one squares, one a day, each in the ink of where the night was: home, away, aboard, in transit, the four night tokens now in `logbook/print_layout.py`; a day the record has no night for shows the paper; the countries of the year with their days as one footer line; no name of a person, a place or an asset, no photo, no map, nothing fetched. One inline SVG drawn in millimetres of A2 and scaled to the sheet, so A2 and A3 are the same drawing; to `--html PATH`, else stdout.
- `logbook digest --paper [--week YYYY-Www | YYYY-MM-DD] [--html PATH | --json]` (`logbook/week_paper.py`, `docs/print.md`): the week as four pages of A4, set from the Year's typographic constants — the days with their one line each (the line `days` prints), the people confirmed present and the week's keepers, the open promises due in the week, and what was read: the browse lines of the week, titles only, never an address. An ISO week, Monday to Sunday; without `--week` the week of the day given, else of today. Composed from the readers, nothing written. Both render from the demo record as fixtures under `tests/fixtures/demo/`, checked structurally.
