### Added
- `export notion` gives each person's `birthday` in the facts, as `logbook person` reads it off a
  resolution line (`YYYY-MM-DD`, or `--MM-DD` without the year), or null; the page has no line for it. A
  birthday binds nothing, so its line passes the gate as a fact's does: one read on a day you have not
  signed stays home until you sign it ([export for Notion](docs/export-notion.md)).
