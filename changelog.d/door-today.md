### Added
- Today, the Library's front door at `/` (RFC 0047 §4.1): the day in words with the place of the night,
  the time and whether it is signed; the photo of the day you starred; one memory, a year ago today (or
  the nearest earlier year that holds a signed reflection, a starred photo or a journey); what you owe
  and what is owed to you, seven at most, the oldest first, each opening its moment; your words, the
  evening's three questions and the Day's Reflect and Sign; and beside them the week's signed days, the
  next four days' calendar, the week's proposal in pencil, and the legend of ink and pencil.
- One header on every Library page (RFC 0047 §6): the wordmark, the four doors (Today, Life, People,
  Places), Ask (a search of the record) and the gear, which opens `/shelves`, every shelf as the front
  page listed them. On a phone the doors are a bar at the foot of the screen. The Library serves its
  own fonts (Instrument Serif, Inter, JetBrains Mono, each with its OFL licence) from the package at
  `/fonts/`, and its policy allows fonts from the server alone.
