### Added
- `logbook demo --years N` (`logbook/demo_life.py`, on the month's `logbook/demo.py`): the Oslo persona's whole life, from her birth `N` years before the day after 30 June 2026 to that day, deterministic from `--seed`, in phases by age that change the shape of the data — a childhood in Bergen of scanned photos, family entries and stories told about her by Kari and Ola (notes in their voice); school, where the calendar fills and Mia and Jens appear, with a phone from 14 (a thin track, iMessage texts, calls, the first flights declared from memory, the family's cabin weeks as trips); university in Copenhagen (lectures with Hanna and Freja, mail, a term in Barcelona of 131 nights); work (meetings, a monthly transcript, mail three times a week, Zürich twice a year, a month in Lisbon, the flight tracker from 30, Ola back at 29); the move to Oslo at 32 (the office, the cabin weekends, the Copenhagen visits); the watch at 34, so health lines start there; the boat at 35 with a July cruise and day sails on its own AIS track; three weeks in Rome at 37; and the default month at full resolution as the last month of a life of 36 years or more. People drift in and out (Jens last heard in 2005, Mia in 2011, the grandmother Solveig in the photos until 2014, Hanna once more in 2019, Ola out through the university years and back). Old years are thin: forty years are about 115,000 lines, every profile present and the chain verifiable; the month record is unchanged. `--days` and `--years` are one or the other. `event`, `message` and `photo` of the story take a `source` or `provenance`; `demo.generate` takes `years`; `demo.write` writes a story to disk. Docs: `docs/demo.md` (the phases, the knobs, the sizes, what to try). Tests (`tests/test_demo_years.py`): every profile and schema on forty years, trips in 2001 and 2025 and the Barcelona term, flights declared in 2000 and tracked in 2025, health from the watch's first day, the move read from the points, a baby's one-year record, `people` with Jens and Mia a decade gone — read from the channels alone, since `people._together` asks for the night of every day against every stay of the window and takes minutes on forty years.
