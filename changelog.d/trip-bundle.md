### Added
- The shared trip (RFC 0025, `docs/trips-shared.md`, `logbook/contrib/trip_bundle.py`). `logbook export trip-bundle <trip-id-or-day> --to <member> [--tier 1|1,2|1,2,3] [--attachments] [--out DIR] [--dry-run]` writes a crossing package (RFC 0005) cut to one trip, under the destination's ceiling in `policy/crossing.json` (ADR 0016): the trip's `photo/v1` lines (hashes; the files only with `--attachments`) and standing `flight/v1` lines verbatim in `entries.jsonl`, the derived rows in `trip.json` (`trip-share/v1`: the stays away from home with their nights and labels, the moves, the flights, the people confirmed present with the refs that name them; never the home stay, never the points), and the resolution lines those refs walk through in `resolution.jsonl`. The people, the sender's own refs and the overlay are tier 2, so the default `--tier 1` crosses places and photos and counts the names held back. Every real export appends a `crossing/v1` line naming the trip (`crossing.record`, now shared with `export crossing`). `logbook import trip-bundle <folder> [--dry-run]` checks every digest and every line's hash, refuses a bundle of the record's own, and appends each line as a received line — kind `received`, source `received`, the sender's line verbatim under `payload.line`, `extra.from` the sender's owner id — plus the page as one received line keyed by the bundle id; a `raw_id` the record already holds, as its own or received before, is skipped and counted (`Index.holders`), and the files go into the store. No derived reader takes the kind, so a received photo is nobody's photo and a received resolution names nobody. `logbook trip <id>` gains `shared`: per sender, who was there according to each record (matched through shared refs, else by name; the owner is `you`), the places the records agree on (within 300 m), and a `seen only by` column, in the text, the JSON and the HTML page; `show` lists a received line as `from <sender>: <kind> <source> · …`. Synthetic fixture: Ines's and Ola's records of one cabin weekend (`tests/cabin.py`); nobody in it exists.
