# ADR 0010 — bighydro is a pen name

Status: accepted · 2026-09-13

**Decision.** The project is stewarded under the pen name bighydro. Anonymity of *data* is strict: nothing personal enters the repository, ever (synthetic fixtures only, scrub before every push). Anonymity of *identity* is soft: the steward does not lead with a legal name and does not link the project from personal accounts, but treats being identified as an inconvenience, not a catastrophe.

**Consequences.** Commits are signed by bighydro's key. The project can accept talks, partnerships or funding later without a crisis of identity.
