# RFC 0036 — payload profile `goal/v1`: a goal in the owner's own words

Status: draft · 2026-10-13 · comment period: two weeks · one of the four profiles of [RFC 0036](../docs/rfcs/0036-goals-reviews-drafts-chapters.md)

What the owner is for, said once in their own words, with the horizon it is for and the day it starts. Not a task (RFC 0016: a task has a due date and a done state), not a note (RFC 0010: a note is anything the owner wrote), not a promise (`docs/promises.md`: a sentence a reader found). One line per goal; a goal reworded is a new line carrying `supersedes`; what became of a goal is a `review/v1` line, never an edit here.

`goal/v1` is a new profile, not one of the RFC 0031 freezes: its schema may change while RFC 0036 is a draft.

## Line

`kind` MUST be `goal`. `tier` MUST be 2. `source` MUST be `manual`: the owner wrote it, not a tool and not a model. `at` is when the owner wrote it; `end` is null. `from`, in the payload, is when the goal starts, which may be before or after `at`.

## Payload

| Field | Type | Req | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| `schema` | `"goal/v1"` | MUST | |
| `text` | string | MUST | the goal, in the owner's words, plain text |
| `horizon` | string | MUST | one of `life`, `year`, `quarter` |
| `from` | `YYYY-MM-DD` | MUST | the first local day the goal holds for |
| `until` | `YYYY-MM-DD` | MAY | the last local day it holds for; absent, it is open until a review closes it or a later goal supersedes it |
| `supersedes` | string | MAY | the id of the earlier `goal` line this one rewords or re-bounds (SPEC §3) |

Anything else MAY be kept under `extra`. A reader ignores what it does not know.

## Tier

2, MUST, by SPEC §4: *notes, decisions, confirmations* are tier 2, and `note/v1` (RFC 0010) is MUST 2 because a note is the owner's words. A goal is the owner's words too, and what the owner wants is at least as private as what the owner did; a goal that reads harmlessly ("sail to Lofoten") sits beside one that does not, and a writer cannot tell them apart. So the tier is the profile's, never a producer's choice, and `logbook add --tier` does not apply to it (§4: an import option sets another tier only where the RFC allows it; this one does not).

## Rules

1. **One line is one goal.** There is no dedupe key from a source: a goal has no `raw_id`, because no app is its source. A writer that is asked for the same text twice writes two goals, and the owner retracts one (RFC 0003).
2. **Rewording supersedes.** A goal said better, or bounded differently, is a new line with `supersedes` naming the earlier one. The **standing goal** is the latest `goal` line not retracted and not named in a later goal's `supersedes` (SPEC §3.2, *standing*). The earlier text is still in the record, and `show` marks it as superseded, as it marks a superseded note.
3. **Nothing here closes a goal.** `until` is a bound the owner set when writing; a goal done, stalled or let go is a `review/v1` line saying so on a date. A reader that lists open goals takes the standing goals whose `until` is absent or not yet past and whose standing review is not `done`, `let go` or `superseded`.
4. **The owner writes it.** `source` is `manual`. A model proposing a goal writes a `draft/v1` or a note labelled `proposal` (`docs/mcp.md`), and the owner writes the goal or does not.

## Example (synthetic)

Ines Nordmann, Oslo, writes the year's goal on the first Sunday of January, for a year that began on the 1st:

```json
{"at":"2026-01-04T10:15:00Z","end":null,"tz":"Europe/Oslo","source":"manual","kind":"goal","tier":2,
 "payload":{"schema":"goal/v1","text":"Sail the boat to Lofoten and back in one summer, without a single night in a marina I didn't choose.",
 "horizon":"year","from":"2026-01-01"}}
```

and the same goal reworded in March, superseding it:

```json
{"at":"2026-03-15T08:40:00Z","end":null,"tz":"Europe/Oslo","source":"manual","kind":"goal","tier":2,
 "payload":{"schema":"goal/v1","text":"Sail to Lofoten and back between June and August, at anchor more nights than not.",
 "horizon":"year","from":"2026-01-01","until":"2026-12-31","supersedes":"019cadd3-6bc0-7dcd-9133-000000000301"}}
```

## What a Day page shows

A goal line is listed on the day its `at` falls on, as every line is (SPEC §3.2.1): `goal  <text>  (year, from 2026-01-01)`, with the mark `superseded` when a later goal names it. The Day derives nothing else from a goal; the goal's verdicts show on the days of their reviews ([review/v1](0036-review-v1.md)), and a list of the standing goals is a reader of its own, not the Day's.

## Notes

- **Why three horizons and not a date.** `until` is the date. The horizon is the kind of goal, which the owner knows when writing and a reader wants to group by: the life's, the year's, the quarter's. A month's goal is a task.
- **Why `from` is separate from `at`.** A goal is often written after it began (the year's, in its first week) and sometimes before (the quarter's, in the last week of the one before). `at` is the act; `from` is the stretch.
